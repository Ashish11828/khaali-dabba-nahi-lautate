# Cinematic polish pass

The original playable story flow is preserved. This pass strengthens the opening, reveal and consequence of each gesture.

- Brushed-steel shader uses view-dependent highlight bands, edge highlights and fine surface grain. It is an artistic approximation for readable metal, not a measured physical material.
- Sharma Aunty's Hindi note is attached to an actual paper card that rises and turns toward the player when the lid opens.
- The three buildings emerge in sequence. `houseRevealStagger` controls their timing. A batched marigold halo and twelve reused sparkles accompany the reveal.
- House targeting lights a doorstep accent rather than changing the building's scale.
- Food plates arc to the three homes. Thank-you cards rise to balconies. For chai, residents walk from their doors into the shared courtyard.
- Windows illuminate in sequence, and opening doors accompany the ending response.
- Shorter ending copy keeps attention on what happens in the neighborhood.

## Checked in Preview

The upgraded scripts and custom shader compile. Pinch-open, all three laddoos, three house stories, gift manipulation, each ending and replay were exercised using SIK preview hand interaction. Chai residents were inspected at two timestamps to confirm movement from their doors to the courtyard. Food and note ending images were visually reviewed.

Physical table detection, on-device readability and sustained performance remain hardware checks. Recorded Hindi narration is still optional and unassigned. No win or production-readiness guarantee is implied.
