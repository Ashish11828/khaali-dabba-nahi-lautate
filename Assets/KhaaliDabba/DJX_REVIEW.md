# DJX narrative review and improvements — 18 October 2026

## Further interaction and story pass

The recipient is now selected explicitly at a glowing doorstep after depositing the gift. The player's warm trail and a named confirmation replace the last-explored inference. They may change recipients before returning the lid. A missing recipient keeps the Lens in selection rather than silently choosing someone.

Resident responses now change articulated props and posture: Meera puts her exercise book aside and leaves her study position for the food, Uncle draws the spare chair closer, and Aunty brings the extra place setting forward. A late cup offered toward the wearer connects the welcoming tradition back to the player. This addresses agency and emotional inclusion; 19/20 was a design aspiration, not a verified judge score.

The benchmark analysis below describes the preceding pass. Its last-observed recipient rule is superseded by explicit selection. The personal-delivery and acknowledgement sequence remains, with the player's invitation added at +11 seconds.

## Weaknesses addressed

| Before | Current behavior | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| All three clues were mandatory | One discovery unlocks gifts; other clues stay available | Let players respond to someone instead of finishing a checklist. |
| Gifts produced three generic positive endings | Most recently explored resident becomes the recipient; every gift has a resident-specific response | Connect observation to a personal choice. |
| All households benefited at once | One plate, note or invitation travels to the chosen resident first | Make the effect of the player's choice visible. |
| Celebration immediately followed lid return | Delivery, recipient response, Aunty acknowledgement, celebration, closing line | Give the emotional change time to register. |
| Replay was available before the payoff | Replay diya appears with the closing line | Avoid accidental interruption of the acknowledgement. |
| Subtitle hints intersected rooftop detail | Subtitle group raised 2 cm | Keep the sentence and spatial consequence visible together. |

There is no correct-gift score. Food, a note and chai are all available for all three residents. The subtitle names the recipient during pickup. Observing another clue before pickup changes the recipient.
